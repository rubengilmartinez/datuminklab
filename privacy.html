Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

DatumInk Lab Publisher (“the app”) is an internal tool operated by DatumInk Lab to publish our own videos to our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts and to read the performance statistics of those accounts. It is not offered to the public and it does not collect data from other people.

What data the app accesses

  • OAuth access tokens for DatumInk Lab’s own accounts, granted by the account owner.
  • Basic profile information of those accounts (account ID, name, handle) to confirm which account is connected.
  • Statistics of our own published videos (views, watch time, likes, comments, shares, saves) to improve future videos.

How it is used and stored

  • Tokens are used only to upload our videos, captions and covers, and to read our own statistics.
  • Tokens are stored on the operator’s own computer, outside any public repository, and are never shared or sold.
  • Statistics are stored in a private database used only to analyse our own content.
  • Videos sent to Instagram are hosted temporarily (signed URL, about one hour) and deleted after publishing.

Platform policies

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings, in Instagram (Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites) and in TikTok (Settings → Security → Apps and services).

Data deletion

Revoking the app’s access in the platform settings stops all access immediately. To request deletion of stored tokens and statistics, write to the contact below; we delete them within 30 days.
